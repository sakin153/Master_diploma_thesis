# Robot Types
- **manipulator**: robotic arms (Franka, UR5, Panda, etc.)
- **quadruped**: four-legged robots (Unitree, Spot, ANYmal, etc.)
- **humanoid**: human-like robots (Atlas, Pepper, etc.)
- **mobile_base**: wheeled platforms (TurtleBot, etc.)
- **other**: drones, grippers, or unspecified robots

# Placement Hints
- **table_mounted**: robot base sits on a table/desk surface
- **floor**: robot stands/moves on the floor
- **wall_mounted**: attached to wall
- **ceiling**: hanging from ceiling
- **unspecified**: placement not mentioned
